FAQ

Before you ask.

What do I actually receive?
The full 4K ProRes or H.264 master of the plate you licensed, delivered by download link — plus a written license certificate with your name, the clip, and the tier.
Can I use a plate in monetized or client work?
Yes — that's what the Commercial tier is for. Personal covers your own non-monetized channels; Extended covers broadcast, film, and use inside products you sell.
Are the plates exclusive to me?
No. Licenses are non-exclusive, which keeps prices low. If you need a scene nobody else has, ask about a custom commission in the request form.
Can you make a custom plate for my project?
Yes. Describe the scene in the Notes field — mood, weather, camera move — and we'll quote a custom render. Typical turnaround is under a week.
How do I pay?
Send the request form and we'll reply within 24 hours with the plate, the license terms, and a payment link. No account needed.

Need a plate that doesn't exist yet?

Describe the scene — mood, weather, camera move — and we'll render it for you. Typical turnaround under a week.
