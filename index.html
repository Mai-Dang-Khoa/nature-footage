    Behind the scene

    From empty level to 4K frame

    Every clip is computer-generated in Unreal Engine 5, a real-time engine — no drones, no location shoots.

    1. 01

      Scene build

      Terrain, rocks and vegetation are assembled in Unreal Engine 5. High-detail geometry keeps close-ups sharp.

    2. 02

      Lighting

      Sun, sky and atmosphere are lit with real-time global illumination, tuned for natural, photoreal light.

    3. 03

      Cinematic camera

      Virtual cameras with real-world lens settings move slowly and smoothly, the way a film crew would shoot.

    4. 04

      4K render

      Final frames are rendered offline at 3840 × 2160, then checked shot by shot before upload.

    Questions

    Can I use these clips on YouTube, in ads or in a documentary?

    All clips are licensed through Adobe Stock, and what you can do depends on the license you pick there. License terms are provided by Adobe Stock — read the Adobe Stock license terms.

    Is this real footage?

    No. Every scene is computer-generated in Unreal Engine 5. Nothing was filmed on location.

    What file will I get?

    Clips are rendered in 4K (3840 × 2160). The exact file format, frame rate and size are listed on each clip's Adobe Stock page before you buy.

    How do I download after buying?

    After you license a clip on Adobe Stock, the full-resolution file is available to download from your Adobe Stock account.
